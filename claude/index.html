How I work

Start from the definition, not the dashboard

Before touching data, agree on what "correct" means. Glossaries, dictionaries, and quality indicators come before reports — otherwise the reports just disagree faster.

Validate before you trust

High-volume healthcare data earns skepticism. Reconciliation and balancing checks are how you catch a problem before it reaches a member or provider, not after.

Translate in both directions

Business requirements become data rules and validation logic. Just as often, data findings need to become plain language a non-technical leader can act on.

Treat AI as a second reviewer, not a shortcut

Current research applies structured human-AI review to stewardship decisions — the aim is a better-checked decision, with a person still accountable for it.
