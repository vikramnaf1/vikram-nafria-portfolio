# Week 3 Loom Talking Points

## 1. Assignment Goal

This portfolio project moved from exploration into finalization. The Week 3 goal was to refine the main portfolio, prepare it for real-world use, and add a mobile-first connect page that can work for networking, career fairs, and NFC sharing.

## 2. Starting Point

I began with several AI-generated versions of the portfolio from earlier weeks. Each version had strengths:

- Replit had the strongest visual identity and felt least like a plain resume.
- Claude and Stitch helped surface alternate structure, mobile ideas, and content patterns.
- Feedback helped identify what needed to be clearer: impact metrics, accessibility, mobile navigation, and proof of data work.

## 3. Final Direction

I selected the architectural-blue version as the final direction because it felt professional, calm, and aligned with healthcare data trust. It uses a restrained palette, clear sections, measurable outcomes, and subtle ambient motion.

## 4. Week 3 Refinements

The homepage was refined for clarity and real-world use:

- Resume links now download a PDF.
- The Impact section uses centered metrics and count-up animation.
- Case studies now use a clearer Problem / Built / Result structure.
- Analytics Lab includes a data-boundary note explaining that synthetic, public, or de-identified data is used.
- A proof-target card was added to show the next inspectable artifact: dataset, notebook or pipeline, and before/after data-quality snapshot.
- A version archive keeps prior design directions accessible.

## 5. Mobile Connect Page

The new `connect.html` page is a mobile-first digital business card. It is designed for quick use in career fairs, networking conversations, and NFC sharing.

It includes:

- Name and positioning
- Quick proof points
- Email
- LinkedIn
- Resume download
- Full portfolio link
- Analytics Lab link

## 6. What I Learned About AI

AI helped generate alternatives quickly, critique the design, and turn feedback into implementation steps. My role was to choose the right direction, decide what matched my professional identity, verify claims, and keep the portfolio focused on trust, healthcare data, and responsible analytics.

## 7. Closing

The final result is not just an assignment page. It is a usable portfolio and mobile contact card that I can share in real-world professional settings.
