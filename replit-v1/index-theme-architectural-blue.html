02 / Case-study depth

Three systems of trust.

Each theme shows the problem, operating model, tools, and business result behind the headline metrics.

MDM + data quality

Definitions before dashboards.

Member, provider, and reference data need shared business meaning before reports can be trusted. I partner with governance and compliance teams on glossaries, dictionaries, quality indicators, and SQL validation across high-volume healthcare datasets.

20%fewer production defects
Privacy + integrity

Useful data without loose controls.

Healthcare analytics has to protect people while still supporting decisions. My work turns HIPAA, PII, and data integrity expectations into validation patterns, metrics, and early signals that teams can act on.

45%fewer privacy incidents
Pipelines + release confidence

Validation that ships with the work.

Across SQL, Ab Initio, SSIS, stored procedures, ESP jobs, API testing, and automated regression checks, I look for the weak point where a release can lose trust and add evidence there. In plain terms: the checks travel with the release, so defects are easier to catch before they become business problems.

70%fewer customer data incidents
